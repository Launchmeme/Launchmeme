How to use Solana Token Creator

Follow these simple steps:

  1. Connect your Solana wallet.
  2. Write the name you want for your Token.
  3. Indicate the symbol (max 8 characters).
  4. Select the decimals quantity (0 for Whitelist Token, 5 for utility token, 9 for meme coins).
  5. Write the description you want for your SPL Token.
  6. Upload the image for your token (PNG).
  7. Put the supply of your Token.
  8. Click on Create, accept the transaction, and wait until your token is ready.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is LaunchMemes.io?
LaunchMemes.io is a non-custodial, no-code software interface that allows users to interact with public smart contracts on the Solana blockchain to create SPL tokens without writing code.

LaunchMemes.io does not issue, endorse, manage, or control any tokens created through the platform. All actions are performed directly on-chain and approved by the user's wallet.
How do I create a token?
To create a token, connect your Solana wallet, fill in the token details (name, symbol, supply, image), and approve the transaction in your wallet.

Once approved, your token is minted directly on the Solana blockchain. Transactions are irreversible and fully controlled by the wallet owner.
Are there any fees?
Yes. The platform charges fixed fees displayed before transaction approval:

• Token creation: 0.1 SOL (+ optional add-ons)
• Copy Trending Tokens: 0.5 SOL (includes revoking Freeze, Mint, and Update Authorities)
• Add liquidity: 0.1 SOL + Raydium/Solana Network fees
• Remove liquidity: 0.1 SOL

All fees are charged only after wallet approval and are non-refundable.
How does liquidity management work on LaunchMemes.io?
LaunchMemes.io provides a non-custodial interface that allows users to interact directly with Raydium smart contracts to add or remove liquidity for their tokens.

LaunchMemes.io does not custody funds, control liquidity, lock pools, or execute transactions on behalf of users. All liquidity actions are executed directly on-chain and approved by the user's wallet.

You can independently verify your liquidity position through Raydium's official pool dashboard (they may have a 10–15 minute delay).

Adding or removing liquidity is entirely controlled by the token creator. Whether you use LaunchMemes.io or Raydium directly, the result is the same: your tokens, your liquidity, fully decentralized.
